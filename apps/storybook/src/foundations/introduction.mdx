# Feather

Feather is a design system that products install as packages and reshape with their brand's tokens, never by editing
its components. It covers three kinds of work: product UI, award-grade websites, and callers (any system that wants a
person to decide something) that send meaning and get the right interface back.

This Storybook is the whole system in one place. Every component renders in every reference brand, every story is
checked for accessibility and for pixel drift, and the pages under **Foundations** explain the values underneath.

## Switch brands

Use the paintbrush **Theme** control in the toolbar. It builds the brand with the production theme engine, so what
you see here is what a product gets. Every story and every swatch on these pages follows it.

| Brand | Schema | Character |
|---|---|---|
| `feather` | `/2` | The default: black and white, JetBrains Mono, color only where it means something. |
| `feather-dark` | `/2` | The same, dark. |
| `paper-sharp` | `/2` | Light, sharp corners, flat. |
| `void-pill` | `/2` | Dark, pill controls, dramatic shadows, snappy. |
| `studio` | `/4` | Dark and cinematic: film grain, a bold display face, the `scout` curve. |
| `portrait` | `/4` | Warm paper, a serif display face, quiet. |
| `seed` | `/4` | Glass overlays, pill shapes, a spring with overshoot. |

The three `/4` brands carry the website layer's expression axes (pace, signature curve, surface, contrast) and the range
layer (loudness, colour blocks, the media scrim, a motion personality). The `/2` brands get the house defaults, so they
work with every website component too.
