# Motion

Feather has two motion vocabularies, and a brand sets both through tokens.

- **Product roles** are for interface: one curve (`--ease-standard`), six durations, a role per use. A component picks
  a role, never milliseconds.
- **Expression roles** are for websites: reveal, media, scene, stagger, hover-out and spring, in the brand's
  **signature curve** and **pace**.

Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and `data-motion="reduced"`. Interaction never waits for motion, and
focus and errors never wait at all.

## Product roles

The brand's `motion` axis (`calm` or `snappy`) scales the six durations. Below, each role plays the move it is made
for.

## Expression roles

Reveal, media and scene take the current brand's duration and curve, and the stagger between blocks. A `/2` brand has no
expression block, so these use the house defaults.

<Unstyled><ExpressionMotion /></Unstyled>

### Pace

`calm` and `snappy` also set the product durations. `cinematic` stretches only the expression roles: product UI stays calm.
Every sequence ends within 1.2 seconds, and a stagger compresses to fit.

<Unstyled><PaceTable /></Unstyled>

## Signature curves

A brand picks one house curve, or a bounded custom `cubicBezier` (x between 0 and 1, y between -0.6 and 1.6; outside
0 to 1 it overshoots, which needs `"overshoot": true`).

## Reduced motion

| Role | Under reduced motion |
|---|---|
| reveal, media, scene | a 150ms cross-fade |
| stagger, hover-out, spring | nothing |
| product roles | transitions end at once (0.01ms) |

A brand may only make a role quieter (`"reduced": { "reveal": "none" }`), never louder.

## Rules

- Animate only `transform`, `opacity`, `filter` and `clip-path`. Never `transition: all`, never width or height.
- Never scale from 0. Resting positions are whole pixels.
- Anything that moves for longer than 5 seconds can be paused.
- One clock drives every scroll, counter and canvas effect, so nothing runs while the tab is hidden.
