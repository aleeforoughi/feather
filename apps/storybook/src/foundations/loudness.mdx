# Loudness

Loudness is one knob, 0 to 3, for how much a surface moves. A brand sets its default (`feather-tokens/4`,
written as `--loudness` on the theme). A page or a block overrides it with `data-loudness="0..3"` on any ancestor, and
a component reads the nearest one.

**The finish is the same at every level.** Anatomy, states, optical alignment, focus, both themes: a loudness 0 page
passes the same detail pass as a loudness 3 one. Only how much moves changes.

## Limits

- **Reduced motion is stronger than any loudness.** A person who asks for less always gets less.
- **The device caps it.** Loudness 3 on a weak phone renders as 2 or 1: the capability gate (cores, memory, data saver,
  a fine pointer, WebGL) decides, and the verdict is remembered for 30 days.
- **Default is 1** (composed): reveals only. A brand opts into more.
